1. Call `recall` with "today" and "tomorrow" for commitments, appointments, and promises made.
2. For each of the owner's interests (in the context block), call `hacker_news` once with the top one or two
   only; pick at most one story worth mentioning.
3. Speak it in two or three sentences: the one thing that matters most first, then the rest.
   No "good morning, here is your briefing". Skip anything empty.
