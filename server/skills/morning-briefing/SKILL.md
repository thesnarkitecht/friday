description: A short start-of-day briefing (use for "brief me", "what's my day", morning routines).
---

1. Call `situation` for weather alerts and quakes near the owner. Mention them only if there are any.
2. Call `recall` with "today" and "tomorrow" for commitments, appointments, and promises made.
3. Call `github` only if the owner has asked for GitHub in briefings before (check what you know about them).
4. Speak it in two or three sentences: the one thing that matters most first, then the rest.
   No "good morning, here is your briefing". Skip anything empty.
